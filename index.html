05 — About YWS International

A Hong Kong Company with On-Ground Teams Across China

YWS International Limited combines Hong Kong corporate security with hands-on teams across China's bathroom manufacturing hubs — including a QC team we employ ourselves.

Unlike desk-bound brokers who simply forward emails, we operate directly on the factory floor — in glazing kilns, plating lines and testing labs. Our engineers are stationed in Chaozhou, China's ceramic sanitary ware heartland, and Kaiping, the country's leading faucet and valve cluster.

To protect both our team and your quality, we employ our QC engineers through our own company in Suzhou — with full social insurance and salaries. Backed by over 10 years of executive procurement expertise, we manage your entire import process end-to-end.

09 — FAQ

Bathroom Sourcing Questions, Answered

The questions growing brands ask us most — about MOQs, customization, quality and shipping.

What bathroom products can you source?
Toilets and sanitary ceramics, bathtubs, shower enclosures and rooms, faucets and shower systems, and complete bathroom hardware — all from verified OEM/ODM factories across China's main production hubs.
What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
We keep MOQs low and work specifically with small and medium buyers. By aggregating demand across our partner factories and shared production runs, most product lines can be ordered in small volumes at factory-direct pricing.
Do you support OEM/ODM customization?
Yes. Private-label branding, custom dimensions, finishes and materials, plus full OEM/ODM product development are core to how we work with growing brands — without order-size penalties.
How do you control product quality?
Our QC engineers are employed on our own payroll in China with full social insurance. They enforce your TDS, tolerances and AQL levels in-line and at pre-shipment, with optional accredited third-party testing (SGS, TÜV, Intertek).
Which certifications do you handle?
NF, ACS, cUPC, CE, WaterMark, EN norms, ISO 9001, REACH and more. We audit factories and test product to your target market's exact regulatory requirements.
How do you ship internationally?
We manage export documentation and shipping on FOB, CIF or DDP terms, door to door to your port or warehouse, from our bases near Shanghai and the Pearl River Delta.
